# Answers Help

## Discover

Browse answered questions to learn what insights others have left or share your knowledge by answering a few questions.

## Search

You can also use the search box on the page to locate questions and answers related to specific words and phrases.

## Ask

What would you like to know? Ask a question on a topic that's important, so that other people can give you answers.

## Tags

Tags are a way of categorizing questions. By adding tags you help others find your questions and get answers more quickly!

## Select the best Answer

Once your question has been answered, you need pick a best answer. When viewing the list of answers, each one will have a button that says "Accept answer". Click it and you're done!

## Answer

Share your knowledge, Help others and be an Expert.

## Tags

Use tags to find open questions related to the topic you know about.

## Search

You can also use the search box on the page to locate questions and answers related to specific words and phrases.

## Best Answer

Your answer to a question may be chosen as the best one, giving you extra points, and bragging rights!
