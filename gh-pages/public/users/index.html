    Hub users

    Hub users

    How to use a hub as a member: your profile and dashboard, the groups and projects you belong to, the resources, publications, and tools you use or contribute, and the places where the community talks.

    Every hub is configured differently, so some features described here may be switched off or named differently on yours. Where a page refers to a menu or tab, it means the one on the hub's site, not the administrator interface.

    Getting started

    • Introduction — what an account gives you and how the member area is laid out.
    • Getting started — registering, your profile, and the dashboard.
    • Member dashboard and Member profile.
    • Registration.

    Working together

    • Groups — creating a group, membership and roles, group pages, the group forum, and group citations.
    • Projects — project files and databases.
    • Publications — managing publications and the curation process.
    • Collections, Citations, Resources, Tools, and Usage.

    Talking and learning

    • Forum, Questions and answers, Blog, Wiki, Knowledge base, Events, Newsletters, and Courses.

    Everything else

    • Search, Tags, Wishlist, Storefront, Support, and the frequently asked questions.
    • Your usage figures and the simulation usage definitions behind them.
