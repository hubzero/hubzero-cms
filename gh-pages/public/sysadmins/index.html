    System administrators

    System administrators

    This book is for the person who looks after the server a hub runs on: the one with a shell on it, who installs the hub, keeps its code current, and puts it back when something goes wrong. Everything here happens on the server, as www-data or as root. The hub's own administrator interface, at /administrator, is the subject of Hub managers.

    • Installing a hub — from a Linux server with nothing on it to a hub with an administrator who can sign in, on Debian 12 and 13, Ubuntu 22.04, 24.04 and 26.04, or Enterprise Linux 8, 9 and 10: PHP 8.2 and MariaDB, Apache or nginx with PHP-FPM or FrankenPHP in Docker, and the installer in the browser, on the command line, or from an answer file.
    • Upgrading a hub — bringing an installed hub up to the head of its release branch, with a backup first and the way back.

    On a small hub the system administrator and the hub manager are often the same person. The split is between the work, not the people: what needs a shell is here, what needs a browser is there.
